How this calculator works

Each month, interest is charged on what you still owe: interest = balance × (APR ÷ 12). Your payment first covers that month’s interest; only what’s left lowers your balance. No new purchases, fees, or late charges are added — life is messier than this page, which is exactly why the real trap is usually worse.

The payment here is a fixed dollar amount each month, the way the Chapter 5 example tells it. Many real cards instead set the minimum as a small percentage of your balance, so the minimum shrinks as you pay — which stretches the payoff out even longer. Either way, the lesson is the same: the minimum is designed to keep you paying, not to get you free.
